## One-line positioning

**{{PRODUCT}} is [the category] for [the audience].**

_Example shape: "Acme Bill is real-time billing infrastructure for AI startups." One sentence. No buzzwords. A stranger should understand who it's for and what it does._

## One-paragraph positioning

_Expand the one-liner to 2-4 sentences. State the outcome the customer gets, not the features. This is the paragraph you reuse in your homepage hero, your LinkedIn bio, and the opening of any article._

## What we are not

A precise list of what you are **not** is as valuable as what you are. It prevents the AI (and your reader) from mis-categorizing you. For each line, name the adjacent thing and the one-sentence reason you're different.

- **Not [adjacent category A].** _Why._
- **Not [adjacent category B].** _Why._
- **Not "the only" option.** There is a real competitive landscape (see `../market/competitors/approved.md`). You win on specific differentiation, not on being alone.
