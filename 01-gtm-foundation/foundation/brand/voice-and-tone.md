# Voice and tone

> **How to fill this in.** Define how your brand sounds so every AI-written piece is consistent. Keep the rules concrete and testable ("no em dashes") rather than vague ("be friendly"). Replace the examples below.

## Voice archetype

Pick one archetype and describe it in 2-3 sentences. The archetype is the personality the AI should imitate.

_Example: "The humble systems engineer. Calm, precise, never hypes. Explains the mechanism, shows the tradeoff, trusts the reader to be smart. Would rather under-claim than over-claim."_

## Tone by context

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Blog / long-form | _e.g. explanatory, evidence-led_ |
| Social (LinkedIn) | _e.g. one sharp idea, first person_ |
| Social (X) | _e.g. punchy, no hashtags_ |
| Docs / product | _e.g. terse, imperative_ |

## Formatting rules (good AI-GTM defaults)

- **Sentence case for all headings.** Never Title Case.
- **No em dashes.** Use a period or a semicolon.
- **Question-based headings where natural.** Helps AI search engines quote you (see `../../03-seo-and-backlinks/docs/seo-optimization.md`).
- **Short paragraphs.** 1-3 sentences. White space is a feature.
- **Currency**: `$1`, `$90`, `~$720` (tilde for estimates).
- **Capitalization**: {{PRODUCT}} always capitalized. AI always uppercase.

## Word blocklist

Never use these (they read as AI-generated marketing filler):

`seamless`, `revolutionary`, `game-changing`, `cutting-edge`, `innovative`, `best-in-class`, `world-class`, `next-generation`, `turnkey`, `paradigm shift`, `unlock`, `leverage` (as a verb), `supercharge`, `effortless`.

Add your own as you spot them.

## Claims discipline

- Position on **differentiation, not superiority**.
- No monopoly claims ("the only", "the first") without a dated, verifiable source.
- Every factual claim about the market or a competitor needs a source. Opinions are fine; dress them as opinions.
