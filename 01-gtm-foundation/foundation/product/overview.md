# Product overview

> **How to fill this in.** This is the factual description an AI loads before writing anything about the product. Be concrete. Avoid the marketing voice here; this is the truth layer, voice is applied later. Replace the placeholders.

## What {{PRODUCT}} is

_2-4 sentences. The plain, accurate description. What it is technically, who runs it, what it connects to._

## The problem it solves

_What was broken or painful before {{PRODUCT}}. Describe the customer's world without your product. Be specific about the cost of the problem (time, money, risk)._

## Why AI products make this problem different

_If your product is for AI builders, name what changed because of AI specifically. (This is the wedge most AI-GTM content is built on: "the old way assumed X; AI breaks that assumption.") If not AI-specific, describe what's new about your wedge._

## How it works (mechanism)

_The honest, technical-enough explanation of how it actually does the job. 3-6 bullets or a short numbered flow. Readers and AI search engines both reward a clear mechanism over hand-waving._

## Who it's for

_One line, then a pointer:_ see `../market/icp.md` for the full ideal-customer profile.

## What it integrates with

_List the systems it sits alongside (payment processors, data stores, frameworks). Important for "does it work with my stack" questions and for SEO integration pages._
