# Content house

> **How to fill this in.** A "content house" is the one big idea your content keeps making, and the few sub-themes ("streams") that support it. Without it, you produce random posts. With it, every piece compounds. Replace the placeholders.

## The big idea

_One sentence your whole content program argues. Not your product pitch, the worldview that makes your product inevitable._

_Example: "Billing built for the subscription era can't survive AI's real-time, per-call economics." Notice it's about the world, not the product. The product is the answer to the idea._

## Content streams

The recurring themes you publish under. Each stream maps to a pillar (from `../brand/messaging-pillars.md`) and an ICP pain (from `../market/icp.md`).

| Stream | The angle | Pillar | ICP pain it speaks to |
|---|---|---|---|
| _e.g. "the real cost of AI"_ | _explainers on unit economics_ | _Observe_ | _"I'm flying blind on margins"_ |
| _e.g. "build vs buy"_ | _honest tradeoff pieces_ | _Monetize_ | _"should I build this myself"_ |

## Funnel awareness (optional)

_If you want to track it: which streams are top-of-funnel (educate strangers) vs bottom-of-funnel (comparison, alternatives, pricing pages that convert). Most teams under-invest in bottom-of-funnel; comparison and "alternatives to X" pages convert best._

## How to use this file

Every article brief should name: which **stream** it belongs to, which **principled position** it reinforces, and which **pillar** it maps to. If a content idea fits none of these, either it's off-strategy or you need a new stream, decide deliberately.
